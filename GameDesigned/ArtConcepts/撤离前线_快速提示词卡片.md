## 负面提示词 (Negative Prompts)

在所有提示词后添加以下内容以避免不想要的效果：

```
--no photorealistic, realistic, high detail, complex textures, smooth surfaces, perfect proportions, anime, cartoon, watercolor, oil painting, sketch, blurry, low quality, asymmetrical, distorted, ugly, deformed
```
